# Market to the woman buying for herself

Jewellery was historically marketed to men buying gifts. Speaking directly to women buying for themselves opens a bigger, repeatable market: self-purchase happens any day, not only on occasions. Copy, engraving and imagery should be about the wearer, not the giver.
