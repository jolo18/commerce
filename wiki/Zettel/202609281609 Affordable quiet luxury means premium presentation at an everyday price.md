# Affordable quiet luxury means premium presentation at an everyday price

In low priced jewellery the products look similar across sellers, so presentation is the differentiator. Look like a 5,000 rupee brand, price at 800 to 1,500. Winning references: Mejuri for presentation, Orelia and Palmonas for price point.
