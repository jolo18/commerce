# In a small catalogue store the homepage is the product page

With one to five products a store is not helping people browse, it is making a case. The homepage should work like a landing page: hook, benefit, proof, objections, then the buy button again.

Consequences:
- All products visible on the homepage without a collection click.
- Price and add to cart reachable without much scrolling.
- Sections that do not push the case get cut.
