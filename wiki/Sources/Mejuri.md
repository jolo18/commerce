# Mejuri

**Why it matters:** Benchmark for everyday fine jewellery presentation and positioning.

## Philosophy
- Fine jewellery as an everyday item, not something saved for occasions.
- Built for women buying for themselves instead of waiting to be gifted. Campaigns: "For My Damn Self", "Buy Yourself the Damn Diamonds". Engravings like "Keep Going" are written for the wearer.
- Brand persona described by the founder as a chic friend who knows a lot more about jewellery.
- Rejects exclusionary luxury rituals: in stores, pieces sit out without glass so people can touch them.

## Positioning
- Accessible luxury: fine materials, clean design, prices well below traditional retail by cutting the usual 8 to 10x markup through DTC.
- Shows an estimated traditional retail price next to its own, so the value is visible.
- Wide price ladder: entry pieces around USD 38 up to engagement rings.

## Customer
- About 94% women, about three quarters buying for themselves. Over half of purchases still self-gifting (2026).
- Repeat customers roughly 40 to 50% of revenue, used by the founder as the main health metric.

## Product and cadence
- Started with weekly drops of 2 to 5 pieces every Monday, on a 3 to 4 week production cycle. Works like a content feed.
- Later expanded materials (silver, vermeil, titanium, lab grown diamonds, steel) only where they fit "intentional, everyday wearable".

## Channel
- From pure DTC to omnichannel: roughly 56 to 61 owned stores, retail about half of revenue. No wholesale, so price and experience stay consistent.
