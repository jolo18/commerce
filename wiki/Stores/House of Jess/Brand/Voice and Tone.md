# Voice and Tone

Short, warm, confident. The stylish friend who knows jewellery.

**Do**
- Talk about wearing, not owning.
- Explain materials and care in plain words.
- Write to the wearer, not the gift giver.

**Don't**
- "Luxury", "exquisite", "stunning", "timeless elegance".
- Exclamation marks, ALL CAPS urgency, fake scarcity.

Principle: [Write the brand as a knowledgeable friend not a luxury house](<../../../Zettel/202609281619 Write the brand as a knowledgeable friend not a luxury house.md>)
