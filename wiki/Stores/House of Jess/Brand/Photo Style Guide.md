# Photo Style Guide

Jose rejected the first direction (linen flat lay test render). Brief: only Indian models and Indian bodies. Proposed direction below, based on the research that follows. Not approved yet.

Common patterns: 4 to 6 images per product; packshot first, on-model second (and as the collection hover); the tight neck-and-collarbone crop with a hand is the category's signature on-model shot; 4:5 portrait for international premium, 1:1 for most Indian brands.

Colourism: ASCI guidelines bar ads that depict darker skin as unattractive or disadvantaged; Tanishq faced a 2018 #ColourMeRight petition for showing only fair-skinned brides.

## Proposed direction: "Warm daylight, real Indian skin"
- **Format:** 4:5 portrait everywhere (gallery, tiles, hover). Hero banners cropped separately for desktop and mobile.
- **Gallery, 6 images per product:**
  1. Packshot: flat, front-on, necklace in a soft U, on warm cream seamless close to Surface #F3EEE6, soft top light, gentle contact shadow.
  2. Worn crop: chin to collarbone, a hand resting at the neck, natural short nails, skin texture kept.
  3. Macro: 3/4 angle on the pendant, shallow depth of field, same cream backdrop.
  4. Portrait: face visible, low 3/4 angle, relaxed and self-assured (not posing for anyone), everyday clothing.
  5. Scale: piece in the palm or between fingers, plus a size graphic (chain length, pendant mm).
  6. Stack or packaging: 2 to 3 pieces layered, or the pouch.
- **Collection tile:** packshot; hover swaps to the worn crop.
- **Casting:** Indian women 22 to 35. A fixed cast of 4 to 5 recurring faces spanning the real range of Indian skin tones (wheatish, dusky, deep), rotated so no product and no page is all fair. Natural hair (open, braid, low bun), minimal makeup, natural or nude nails, visible skin texture, no heavy retouching.
- **Wardrobe:** everyday Indian city wardrobe in brand colours: cream, ink, plum, brown, olive. Cotton shirts, fitted tanks, solid kurtas with V or boat necklines, the odd solid saree blouse. No bridal or festive heaviness, no logos, no busy prints.
- **Light and grade:** soft window daylight, warm but neutral whites, cream matched to #F3EEE6, no pink cast, no heavy filters.
- **Sets:** plain warm plaster wall, cream seamless, the occasional ink or plum band for contrast. No satin, petals, glass, pebbles or pink props.
- **Never:** sale ribbons over photos, mixed backdrops within one gallery, lightened skin.
