# Brand Brief

Modelled on Mejuri's positioning, written in our own words. See [Mejuri](<../../../Sources/Mejuri.md>).

- **Positioning:** Fine-looking jewellery for everyday life, priced so you can buy it for yourself, today, for no reason.
- **Audience:** Women 22 to 35 in Indian cities. Self-purchase first, gifting second.
- **Price ladder:** entry about ₹499, core ₹799 to ₹1,299, hero pieces up to ₹1,999.
- **Philosophy:** No occasion needed. Wear it daily, build your stack over time.
- **Persona:** The stylish friend who knows jewellery and tells it straight.
- **Cadence:** Launch with 5 pieces, then a small drop of 2 to 3 pieces every month.
- **Feel (placeholder):** warm, effortless, considered.
- **Tagline shortlist:** For no occasion. / Yours, every day. / Bought by you. (final pick pending)
- **Launch set:** pending.
