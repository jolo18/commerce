# Typography

Exploration boards T1 to T5 on the design canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/5TLcsPGoEAqMPBkJSkctYb (row "Typography on House Evolved"). All fonts are in the Shopify font library, so no custom font files.

| # | Name | Heading | Body | Character |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Editorial Garamond | EB Garamond 500 | Karla 400 | Bookish, warm, knowledgeable |
| T2 | Soft Modern | Fraunces 300 (fraunces_n3) | Manrope 400 (manrope_n4) | Contemporary, soft curves |
| T3 | Fashion Didone | Bodoni Moda 400 | Jost 400 | Magazine luxury, least casual |
| T4 | Engraved Classic | Marcellus (marcellus_n4) | Karla 400 (karla_n4) | Jeweller's engraved capitals |
| T5 | Clean Sans | Jost 400, spaced caps | Jost 400 | Minimal, most neutral |

Scale: H1 56 / 36 mobile, H2 32 / 26, H3 22 / 20, body 16, eyebrow 12 caps.
Current store uses Avenir Next Rounded (Sense). Unverified handles (EB Garamond, Bodoni Moda, Jost) get confirmed in D2.

## Decision
**T2 Soft Modern: Fraunces over Manrope.** See [005 Typography](<../Decisions/005 Typography.md>).
