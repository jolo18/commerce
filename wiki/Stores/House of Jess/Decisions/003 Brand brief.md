# 003 Brand brief

## Context
Needed positioning and voice before palette, type and imagery. Jose asked to model it on Mejuri. Verbatim copying was ruled out (copyright, passing off), so the brief adapts Mejuri's philosophy in original words.

## Options
1. v1: everyday gold, "Gold for every day" taglines.
2. v2: no gold framing, self-purchase and no-occasion philosophy.

## Decision
v2, see [Brand Brief](<../Brand/Brand Brief.md>). Tagline shortlist kept open.
