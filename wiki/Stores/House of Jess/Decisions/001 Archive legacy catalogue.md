# 001 Archive legacy catalogue

## Context
The store held 58 products from 2024 to 2025: colourful crystal and American diamond statement sets, earrings and adjustable rings. The new direction is minimal gold demi-fine in a Mejuri-like presentation, and the old catalogue clashes with it. Data quality was also poor (wrong product types, HEIC images, copy-paste descriptions).

## Decision
Archive the full legacy catalogue. Archiving is reversible.

## Why
[202609281602 Sell one idea not a catalogue](<../../../Zettel/202609281602 Sell one idea not a catalogue.md>) · [202609281603 A small brand looks established through restraint not decoration](<../../../Zettel/202609281603 A small brand looks established through restraint not decoration.md>)

## Affects
- Shopify: 58 products ARCHIVED.
- Data: `store.json` summary and `launch_candidates`.
- Open: launch set to be chosen in A1.
