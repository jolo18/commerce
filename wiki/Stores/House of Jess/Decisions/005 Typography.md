# 005 Typography: Fraunces over Manrope

## Context
Five pairings from the Shopify font library were compared on the House Evolved palette (hero, product page, type scale). See [Typography](<../Brand/Typography.md>).

## Decision
T2 Soft Modern.
- Headings: Fraunces 300 (`fraunces_n3`), sentence case, -0.01em.
- Body and UI: Manrope 400 (`manrope_n4`), 500 for emphasis (`manrope_n5`).
- Eyebrows: Manrope 12px caps, 0.16em tracking, plum #6B2F5B.
- Scale: H1 56/36, H2 32/26, H3 22/20, body 16, small 14.

## Why
Warm and current, soft curves carry over some of the old rounded feel without looking playful. Fits "warm, effortless, considered" and the knowledgeable-friend voice.

## Affects
- Data: `brand.json` typography.
- A4: wordmark set in Fraunces.
- D2: theme heading and body font settings.
