# 006 Logo: Jess.

## Context
Round 1 explored five "House of Jess" wordmarks. Jose asked for the logo to show just "Jess". Round 2 (J1 to J5) was checked on full desktop and phone homepage mocks. See [Logo and Assets](<../Brand/Logo and Assets.md>).

## Decision
J5: *Jess* in Fraunces 300 italic, followed by an upright full stop in plum #6B2F5B on light backgrounds (same colour as the wordmark on dark). Small mark: italic J with the plum stop.

Store name stays **House of Jess** (assumed, not yet confirmed); the logo reads "Jess."

## Why
The full stop echoes the tagline "For no occasion." and gives the brand a small repeatable detail. Italic Fraunces keeps it personal without a script font.

## Affects
- Data: `brand.json` logo.
- Next: outlined SVG and PNG exports, favicon, social share image (before D1).
- Open: trademark check on "Jess" before printing packaging.
