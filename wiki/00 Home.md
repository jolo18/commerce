## How this vault works
- **Inbox/**: fleeting notes. Process weekly into a Zettel, a Decision or delete.
- **Zettel/**: one reusable idea per note, true for any store. Filename `<YYYYMMDDHHMM> <claim>.md`.
- **Sources/**: notes on reference stores and articles.
- **Maps/**: index notes grouping Zettel by theme.
- **Playbooks/**: step by step procedures built from Zettel.
- **Stores/**: store specific decisions, brand guides, designs, products.
- **Templates/**: note templates (Core plugin: Templates).

Every approved decision cites the Zettel behind it, and new reusable insight becomes a Zettel in the same commit.
